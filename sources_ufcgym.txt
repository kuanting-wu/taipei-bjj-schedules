Scope / branches
- Official Taiwan site lists two Greater Taipei branches: 台北-敦南旗艦館 (Dunnan, 大安區安和路一段27號 B1) and 台北-內科模範館 (Neihu, 內湖區洲子街55號 1F). It also lists a Taichung branch, excluded.
